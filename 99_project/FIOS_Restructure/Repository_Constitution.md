# FIOS Repository Constitution

## Article 1 — Purpose

FIOS exists as one connected operating system.

Every folder, file, module and class must contribute to that purpose.

---

## Article 2 — Simplicity

- Prefer fewer folders.
- Prefer fewer files.
- Prefer reusable modules.
- Avoid unnecessary abstraction.

---

## Article 3 — One Responsibility

Every module has one clear responsibility.

If two modules perform the same responsibility:

- Merge them.

---

## Article 4 — Connected Repository

Nothing exists independently.

Every module must be:

- Referenced
- Reachable
- Documented
- Connected

---

## Article 5 — Repository Brain

Before creating, moving, merging or deleting anything:

Repository Brain analyzes.

Repository Brain recommends.

Developer approves.

Only then changes are applied.

---

## Article 6 — Safe Evolution

No automatic deletion.

Workflow:

Analyze

↓

Recommend

↓

Backup

↓

Approve

↓

Apply

↓

Compile

↓

Test

↓

Commit

---

## Article 7 — Living Architecture

Architecture evolves.

Complexity does not.

Every evolution must reduce or maintain complexity.

---

## Article 8 — Builder AI

Builder AI shall:

- Understand the repository.
- Improve the repository.
- Reuse existing modules.
- Minimize new files.
- Keep architecture clean.

---

## Article 9 — Dashboard

Dashboard never owns logic.

It visualizes Repository Brain.

---

## Article 10 — Final Principle

The repository is a living system.

Every change must make it:

- Smaller
- Cleaner
- Faster
- Easier to understand
- Easier to automate
- Easier to evolve
